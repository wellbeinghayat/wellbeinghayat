FAQ

Questions, answered

Before you download

What does WhereIParked actually do?

It remembers where you parked — automatically, using your phone's location and motion sensors, or with a single manual tap — and gives you one-tap directions back when you need them.

Is it free?

Yes, free to download and use. If we ever add optional paid features, anyone who's already downloaded the app keeps everything free for life.

Does it drain my battery?

It's built to run in the background using the same low-power location and motion APIs your phone already relies on — designed specifically to be light on battery.

Does it work without an internet connection?

Yes — detecting, saving, and navigating back to your spot all work with GPS alone. A connection is only used to show a readable street address instead of raw coordinates.

Do I need to create an account?

No. There's no sign-up and no login — everything works from the moment you install it.

Installation & troubleshooting

How do I install WhereIParked?

Once it's live, download it from the App Store on iOS or Google Play on Android — links will appear at the top of this page.

Why does the app need location permission?

Location is how WhereIParked knows where you parked and where you are now, so it can guide you back. It's used only for this — never sold or shared.

The app isn't detecting my parking spot — what do I do?

Check that background location access is set to "Always" (iOS) or "Allow all the time" (Android) in your phone's Settings — automatic detection needs it to work while the app isn't open.

How do I enable background location access?

iOS: Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → WhereIParked → Always. Android: Settings → Apps → WhereIParked → Permissions → Location → Allow all the time.

How do I delete my data?

Your saved spot, photo, and notes live only on your device. Clear them anytime from within the app, or uninstalling the app removes everything.
